# Fox-Hunt transmitter-for FM SSB Transceivers -ESP32-Dev-kit-V1

This is a fox hunt transmitter with a WebGUI, you can upload both ADPCM and unsigned WAV files in the WebGUI

there is a Morse message entry where you can put in your callsign and whatever message you want to add.

then there is a moment of silence entry to add space in between your foxhunt transmitter voice and morse messages

all entries will appear in the playlist where you can then move your entries around or delete when editing your playlist.

This enables the ESP32 output an analog signal in morse tones and voice playback where you feed into the microphone 
input of the transceiver of your choice, that can be a VHF/UHF handheld, or a HF transceiver that can have
AM, FM, SSB modes. Take care if you use AM, spectral emission limits of AM is 5KHz wide, so make sure you process your
audio to limit to 2.5KHz or less top end frequency response.
